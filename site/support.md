## Getting started

1. Open **Models** from the name at the top of a chat and download one. LFM3 1.7B is a good first choice. If your device has Apple Intelligence, you can use that with nothing to download.
2. Ask something. If the answer needs the web, the agent searches and shows you the steps it took.
3. To let it use a service such as Linear, open **Settings > Connectors**, add the service and sign in.

## Common questions

**A model will not download or load.** Check free storage in Settings. Larger models need a recent device; the Models screen marks which ones fit yours.

**A reply stopped when I left the app.** A model that runs on your device pauses when Resident is not on screen and carries on when you come back.

**The agent did not use a connector.** Check that the connector is switched on for the agent you are using, under **Settings > Agents**. Naming the service in your message ("in Linear") helps.

**How do I delete everything?** Deleting the app removes your chats, memory, skills and downloaded models from the device.
