Why it works

Built for how small models actually behave.

A model that fits on a phone is capable and easily overwhelmed. Give it a long prompt, fifty tools and a page of raw data and it falls over. Most of Resident is the work of not doing that.

Tools appear when they are needed

Describing every tool of a real service takes more text than a phone-sized model can hold. Resident tells the model each connector exists in one line, then opens only the few tools a request calls for. Connect several services and the model still sees a short, clear list.

Answers from tools are made to fit

Ask for your issues and a service sends twenty, each with pages of description. Cut that off and the model sees five and goes round in circles. Resident shortens the long parts first, keeps every item, and says plainly when something was left out.

The chores are done for it

A small model busy answering will not stop to remember a fact about you, name the chat or tidy a long conversation. Resident does each as a short request of its own after the reply, so they happen every time.

It keeps its place

Leave the app mid-answer and the reply holds where it is, then carries on from the same word when you come back. Long chats are summarised behind the scenes, so the tenth question is as quick as the first.

You see every step

Each search, page and tool call is shown as it happens, with what was sent and what came back. When an answer is wrong you can see where it went wrong.

Nothing downloads that will not run

Before a model downloads, Resident works out the memory it needs at full context and checks it against your device. Paste any MLX model from Hugging Face and get a straight answer first.

What it does

An agent, not a chat box.

Looks things up

Searches the web and reads pages when a question needs something recent.

Works it out

Runs code in a sandbox for arithmetic, dates and data, so answers are computed, not guessed.

Uses your apps

Calendar and Reminders on the device. Linear, Notion and anything else that speaks MCP, by pasting an address.

Remembers you

Keeps a short list of lasting facts you mention. Read it, edit it or switch it off.

Learns your way

Turn something it just did into a skill with one tap. You see and approve every skill before it is kept.

Reads what you give it

Photos, PDFs and documents, and dictation by a speech model that also runs on the device.

Agents

Opinionated, and yours to change.

An agent is a set of instructions, the tools it may use and how hard it thinks. Start with the Assistant, switch to the Coder, or make your own for the things you do often.

The defaults are meant to be right. The numbers are there when you want them: temperature and reply length per agent, sampling per model, thinking per chat.

Automation

Runs from Shortcuts, in the background.

The Ask Resident action takes a message, an agent and a model, and hands back the reply as plain text. A model on your phone answers without the app ever opening. Chain it with anything, or run it on a schedule.

Jobs that take a while show their progress as a Live Activity, with a button to stop them.

Private because it is local.

On your device
